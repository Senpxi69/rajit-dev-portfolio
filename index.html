About

Rajit Dev

He doesn't just choreograph movement. He creates moments.

Rajit Dev is a choreographer and director with a distinct creative vision, bringing together movement, music, and storytelling to create work that connects with audiences. Whether it's a music video, a live performance, or a cinematic concept, he believes in pushing boundaries and making every frame count.

Fluent in Salsa, Bollywood Jazz, Hip-hop and Contemporary, he blends them into modern, catchy hook steps that are easy to learn and hard to forget, which is why millions of Reels remakes exist. He choreographs for the camera and the crowd at once, from viral tracks to the FIFA Fan Festival Qatar 2022 stage.

After more than a decade, he still works the same way: feel the music first, then let the body answer. For events and shows he brings a team of dancers trained under him, who know his style and his counts, so every stage looks sharp and in sync.

  • StyleBollywood Jazz, Hip-hop, Contemporary, Salsa
  • SignatureCatchy, modern hook steps
  • EnergyPlayful, sharp, built for the camera
  • TeamTrained dancers for events & shows
  • ReachMillions of Reels remakes
